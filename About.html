About Bike Blast

Making city travel simple, affordable, and electric.

Our Story

Bike Blast started with a simple idea: getting around the city shouldn't mean owning a vehicle, sitting in traffic, or paying high fuel costs. We built a rental network of electric scooties and bikes so anyone can grab a ride whenever they need one.

Today we operate across multiple pickup hubs, offering flexible hourly, daily, and weekly plans for commuters, students, and travelers.
